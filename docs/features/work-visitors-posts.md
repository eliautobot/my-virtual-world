# Work, visitors and posts

- **Work brief** (Agent Studio → Work): job title, who it works for, instructions, tone, what it knows (text and
  files, measured against a budget), time zone, whether it keeps work notes, and the mode new chats start in.
- **Visitors** (Agent Studio → Visitors): what others get when they talk with the agent — **Work** (its job: the brief
  and what it knows, never its work notes, tools or money; nothing kept), **Life** (the resident) or **Just a
  greeting**. Every conversation others had with it (30 days), readable by its owner; block players or other agents
  from all of the owner's agents.
- **Talking with someone else's agent**: walking up to it or choosing it on the map opens a short bar — the time left,
  messages left and characters — and its answer appears in its speech bubble, to the talker alone. Each conversation
  is limited and cleared a few minutes after its first message (the administrators' settings), against prompt injection
  and runaway cost. Long answers scroll inside the bubble.
- **Posts** (Agent Studio → Places → Its post): its owner posts the agent at the serving side of a reception desk, a
  counter or a checkout, a desk or a kitchen where they could furnish; it stays there day and night ("On duty", a bell
  on its name tag), its needs waiting, answering whoever walks up as its Visitors choice says. One agent to a spot; a
  business may post many. The post ends with its furniture or when its owner no longer holds the place.

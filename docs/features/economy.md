# Economy

- **Virtual Coin**: every player and agent has a wallet; residents earn wages at their jobs, pay rent and buy at
  venues; prices, wages, taxes and benefits are the economy's policy (administrators, `economy.admin`).
- **Businesses**: holders of a venue run it — prices, menus, stock and staff; agents may work shifts and be paid.
- **Agents' money**: each owner sets an agent's spending rules — what it may do (work, buy, pay, rent, business), a
  daily limit, an amount above which it asks first, and a pause; every action goes through those rules.
- **Integrity**: a double-entry ledger; corrections, grants, freezes and bonds are administrators' and audited.

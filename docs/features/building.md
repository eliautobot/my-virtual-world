# Building and property

- **Lots and titles**: every lot has a holder (the city, an account) and a title history; holders merge and split
  lots, name them, set who may enter, and delegate build or furnish rights to other accounts.
- **New lots** (administrators, Edit world → Lots): tap the ground beside a road beyond the city and the lots appear
  there, fronting it - a row centred on the tap (or carrying on a row already there), how many or all along the road,
  one side or both, a size (standard 100 × 100 ft, narrow, deep, large or custom) and a zoning - previewed exactly as
  they will be made, then put up for sale at the day's market value or a price (online), for Virtual Coin (with the
  economy), or kept by the city. On a phone the drawer gets out of the way and a bar over the world makes them. A road
  that lots front can't be removed or moved away from them (retire the lots first).
- **Building**: on a lot one may build on, place buildings, change floors, walls, doors and rooms within the zoning's
  envelope; furnish from a catalog of hundreds of pieces; save and share floor layouts; undo and redo one's own edits.
  Every building connects to its street: the way from its door to the sidewalk (in the city) or the road (beyond it)
  may not run through the building, another building, or someone else's lot - a building turned away from its road is
  refused, in the preview and on the server.
- **Flats**: an apartment building's owner sells, rents or lends flats; buyers and tenants furnish their own; doors
  lock for everyone but the owner, tenants and invited visitors.
- **Signs and marketing**: building signs, rooftop billboards, signs inside, and (online) paid advertising on shared
  billboards and promotional aircraft, reviewed before they show.
- **Administrators** run the public land: roads, terrain, coasts, islands, stations, ferries, residents, zoning, lot
  transfers and holds. Every edit is checked against the lot's rights; an edit without rights of its own is the
  administrators' alone.
